# Privacy notice

MX Click Haptics processes left and right mouse-button events locally on the Windows computer where it runs. The desktop app communicates with the Logitech Plugin Service through a local named pipe and saves its settings to a local JSON file.

The app and plugin do not send mouse events, settings, diagnostics, or other usage data to a remote server. They do not include analytics or telemetry.

This notice describes the current implementation. It must be reviewed and updated if a future version adds network communication, analytics, or other personal-data processing.
